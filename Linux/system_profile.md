## Binary Number Representation

### Decimal 75 to Binary

Divide the decimal number by 2 repeatedly and record the remainder at each step.

| Division | Quotient | Remainder |
|---|---:|---:|
| 75 ÷ 2 | 37 | 1 |
| 37 ÷ 2 | 18 | 1 |
| 18 ÷ 2 | 9 | 0 |
| 9 ÷ 2 | 4 | 1 |
| 4 ÷ 2 | 2 | 0 |
| 2 ÷ 2 | 1 | 0 |
| 1 ÷ 2 | 0 | 1 |

Reading the remainders from bottom to top:

**75₁₀ = 1001011₂**

### Binary 110101 to Decimal

Using positional weights:

110101₂ = (1 × 2⁵) + (1 × 2⁴) + (0 × 2³) + (1 × 2²) + (0 × 2¹) + (1 × 2⁰)

= 32 + 16 + 0 + 4 + 0 + 1

**110101₂ = 53₁₀**

### Why Computers Use Binary

Computers use binary internally because digital electronic circuits can reliably represent two states, on and off, corresponding to 0 and 1.
